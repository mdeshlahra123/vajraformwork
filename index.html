On site

Handling and installation, step by step.

  1. Confirm panel and use

    Check the core type and thickness are right for the element you're casting.

  2. Follow the support layout

    Set props, runners and battens to the approved product instructions and your project's formwork design.

  3. Inspect before casting

    Check panels, joints and supports are secure and level before the pour.

  4. Strip and clean

    De-shutter by hand, don't drop or lever panels off. Clean as per supplier guidance.

  5. Inspect and store

    Check for damage, then stack flat in the shade until the next pour.

Support spacing and assembly must follow the product's approved instructions and your engineer's formwork design. Ask us for the installation guidance for the variant you buy.

Seedhi baat

What contractors worry about, answered straight.

"Garmi mein jhuk jayega?"

Plastic panels need the right support layout. Follow the approved spacing for the panel and slab, and store panels flat in the shade, not stacked in direct sun for days.

"Kinare toot jaate hain."

Edges get damaged when panels are thrown from the slab or levered off with a crowbar. Strip by hand and stack flat.

"Kil ke chhed ka kya?"

Nailing through the same spot again and again weakens it. Shift nail positions between pours.

"Sasta ply hi theek hai."

For one house with two slabs, it may be. PP makes sense when you cast again and again. Try the calculator with your own numbers.

"Quality ka bharosa kaise karein?"

See the stock on a video call, start with a small sample order and try it on one pour. Ask what technical data we have for your variant. Then decide on bulk.

"Rate badal jayega?"

PP prices move with raw material costs. Your quotation states the price and how long it's valid.

FAQ

Questions contractors ask us

Which sizes and variants do you have?

Round-hole and square-hole PP panels. Sizes and thickness depend on current stock, so send us your requirement and we'll confirm what's available.

How many times can I reuse a panel?

It depends on the variant, how it's handled, how often it's cut, cleaning and site conditions. Strip by hand, don't drop panels from height, and store them flat. Ask us about the data we have for your variant.

How do I choose the right panel?

Tell us what you're casting (slab, beam, column or wall) and the thickness. We'll suggest a core type and thickness. Support layout must follow the product instructions and your formwork design.

What technical documents do you have?

Ask us on WhatsApp. We'll share what's available for the variant you're buying.

Can I use my existing props and runners?

PP panels are used with conventional props, runners and battens. Spacing must follow the product's instructions, which may differ from what you use for ply.

Do I need shuttering oil?

Follow the supplier's recommended release procedure for your concrete mix and finish. We'll share it with your order.

How do I check stock?

Message us on WhatsApp. We'll confirm availability and can show you the panels on a video call.

Can I start with a sample order?

Yes. Start small, try the panels on one pour, then decide on the full quantity.

How is freight worked out?

By your site location and the quantity. We quote panels and freight together, so you see the delivered cost.

How do payment and invoicing work?

NEFT, RTGS or UPI. Every order gets a GST invoice.
