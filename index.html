Choose your core

Two panel types. Pick by the job.

Round hole

Lighter, easy to handle

Round tubes inside give a good strength-to-weight balance. The usual choice for slabs, beams and general RCC work.

Square hole

Stiffer, for heavier loads

A grid core that resists bending more. Better for shear walls, thick slabs and places where the concrete pressure is high.

On site

Same system your team already knows

  1. Supports across the holes

    Runners and battens go perpendicular to the hole direction of the panel, never along it.

  2. Slab: 30–35 cm spacing

    Keep support spacing under the slab deck within 30–35 cm centre to centre.

  3. Walls: 20–25 cm spacing

    Shear walls carry more concrete pressure, so tighten support spacing to 20–25 cm.

  4. No oil. Just wash.

    No shuttering oil needed. After de-shuttering, wash with water and stack flat for the next pour.

Seedhi baat

What contractors worry about, answered straight.

"Garmi mein jhuk jayega?"

Not if the supports are right. Keep 30–35 cm spacing under slabs and 20–25 cm on walls, and store panels flat in shade. The panel is rated for working temperatures up to 90 °C.

"Kinare toot jaate hain."

Edges get damaged when panels are thrown down from the slab or levered off with a crowbar. Strip by hand, stack flat. We'll show your labour the right way on the first pour.

"Kil ke chhed ka kya?"

Nailing through the same spot repeatedly weakens it. Shift nail positions between pours. Small holes don't show on the concrete face.

"Sasta ply hi theek hai."

For one house with two slabs, it may be. PP pays off when you cast again and again. Use the calculator above with your real numbers.

"Quality ka bharosa kaise karein?"

See the stock on a video call, start with a small sample order, and ask for the test certificate. Cut it, stand on it, leave it in water. Then order in bulk.

"Rate badal jayega?"

PP raw material prices move with crude oil. We quote a fixed price valid for the dates written on your quotation.

FAQ

Questions contractors ask us

How many times can I reuse a panel?

50 or more with proper handling. Strip by hand, don't throw panels from height, wash after every pour and store flat. Panels that get cut often for small areas will last fewer cycles.

What's the minimum order?

No fixed minimum. You can start with a small sample order. Larger and repeat orders get better pricing.

How long does delivery take?

We dispatch from ready stock. Transit time depends on your state. We'll tell you the expected date with the quote.

Can I use my existing props and runners?

Yes. Panels sit on the same props, runners and wooden battens you use with plywood.

Do I need shuttering oil?

No. PP doesn't bond to concrete the way wood does. Just wash the panels with water after each de-shuttering.

Round hole or square hole, which one?

Round hole for most slabs and beams. Square hole when you need extra stiffness, like shear walls or thick slabs. Tell us the job and we'll suggest one.

Can panels be cut?

Yes, with a normal circular saw or hand saw. Cut pieces can be reused for beam sides and smaller areas.

Do you deliver to my state?

Yes, we deliver across India. WhatsApp us your site location and quantity and we'll quote the panels and freight together.

Is GST invoice available?

Yes, every order is billed with a proper tax invoice.
