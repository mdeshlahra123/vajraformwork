Seedhi baat

What contractors worry about, answered straight.

"Garmi mein jhuk jayega?"

Plastic softens with heat. That's why early plastic boards in India got a bad name. Keep props and runners at the spacing we give you for your slab thickness, and don't leave panels stacked in direct sun for days. We'll check the first layout with you.

"Kinare toot jaate hain."

Edges get damaged when panels are thrown down from the slab or levered off with a crowbar. Strip by hand, stack flat. We'll show your labour the right way on the first pour.

"Kil ke chhed ka kya?"

Nailing through the same spot repeatedly weakens it. Shift nail positions between pours. Small holes don't show on the concrete face.

"Sasta ply hi theek hai."

For one house with two slabs, it may be. PP pays off when you cast again and again. Use the calculator above with your real numbers.

"Quality ka bharosa kaise karein?"

Fair question. Ask for a sample panel. Cut it, stand on it, leave it in water. We'd rather you test it than trust a brochure.

"Rate badal jayega?"

PP raw material prices move with crude oil. We quote a fixed price valid for the dates written on your quotation.

06 / FAQ

Questions contractors ask us

How many times can I reuse a panel?

It depends on handling, cleaning and how often panels are cut. We're documenting real counts on local sites and will share actual data, not a brochure number.

Can I use my existing props and runners?

Yes. Panels sit on the same props, runners and wooden battens you use with plywood.

Do I need shuttering oil?

PP doesn't bond to concrete the way wood does, so many sites skip it. We'll advise based on your mix and finish needs.

Can panels be cut?

Yes, with a normal circular saw or hand saw. Cut pieces can be reused for beam sides and smaller areas.

Do you deliver to my state?

Yes, we deliver across India. WhatsApp us your site location and quantity and we'll quote the panels and freight together.

Is GST invoice available?
