Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through our website, WhatsApp, or a Facebook/Instagram lead form, we may collect your name, phone number, city, business type (contractor, dealer, builder or homeowner), approximate area required and expected timeline.

How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry: sending rates, sizes, stock details and delivery information, usually on WhatsApp or by phone. We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

Sharing

We share details only when needed to fulfil your order (for example with a transport partner for delivery) or when required by law.

Storage

Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to serve you and maintain business and GST records.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you or to delete your details at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 83050 70160.
